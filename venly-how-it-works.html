Each listing has a contact form where you can send your details and event information directly to the host. They'll typically get back to you within 24 hours. All communication is between you and the host — Venly isn't in the middle.
Venly lists three types of spaces: Event Spaces (for weddings, parties, launches, galas and more), Meeting Spaces (boardrooms, workshops, conference rooms), and Shoot Locations (for film, photography and content creation).
