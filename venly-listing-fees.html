Sign up, choose your plan, and list your space. People browse Venly to find spaces that suit their needs and contact you directly through the platform. No commission on bookings — just a flat monthly fee.
Choose the category that best describes the primary use of your space. Event Spaces are for larger gatherings and functions. Meeting Spaces are for boardrooms and smaller professional settings. Shoot Locations are for photography and film productions.
You can manage your listing at any time from your account dashboard. To take a live listing down, go to My Listings, select the space, and choose Cancel subscription. You can either keep it live until the end of the period you've already paid for, or take it down straight away — either way it moves back into draft mode, so it's no longer visible on Venly and you won't be charged again.

If you decide to list your space again in the future, simply return to your drafts and republish it. To permanently remove a listing, delete it from your drafts and it'll be removed from your account completely.
