About us

While planning a business event and a 21st birthday, I realised something was missing from the experience: excitement. I wanted the search for a space to feel fun, easy, and full of possibility — not like ticking a chore off a list. That's what sparked the idea for Venly: a bright, user-friendly platform designed to make discovering amazing spaces feel effortless and enjoyable.

Venly is built for both those looking for spaces and those listing them. We believe in fairness, simplicity, and keeping things clutter-free. No ads, no stress — just a smooth, easy way to find the perfect space for any occasion.
